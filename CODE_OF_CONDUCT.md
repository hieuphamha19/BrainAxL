# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Participants must communicate constructively, welcome good-faith questions,
respect differing experience levels, and avoid discriminatory, threatening,
or personally identifying content.

Project discussions must not include patient-identifying information,
restricted challenge data, credentials, or private correspondence. Technical
disagreement is expected; personal attacks and sustained disruptive behavior
are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community or sensitive data.
Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through their GitHub
profiles. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
