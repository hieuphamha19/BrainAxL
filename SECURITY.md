# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature from the
repository **Security** tab. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability,
credential exposure, unsafe deserialization path, or potential disclosure of
sensitive medical data.

Include the affected revision, environment, reproduction steps, expected
impact, and any suggested mitigation. Maintainers will acknowledge a complete
report as soon as practical and coordinate disclosure after a fix is ready.

## Research and clinical-use notice

BrainAxL is research software. It has not been cleared or approved as a medical
device and must not be used for clinical diagnosis or treatment decisions.
Users are responsible for validating models in their own setting and complying
with data-governance, privacy, and challenge-dataset terms.

Only the latest `main` branch and tagged public releases are supported. Model
checkpoints and third-party dependencies retain their own security and license
considerations.
